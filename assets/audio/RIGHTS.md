# PodFlow brand audio rights

`podflow-intro.wav`, `podflow-transition.wav` and `podflow-outro.wav` are original synthesized assets generated by `scripts/generate_brand_audio.py` for PodFlow Studio. They do not contain third-party recordings, samples, loops or model output.

Copyright © 2026 PodFlow Studio contributors. These files are distributed with this project under `LGPL-3.0-only`. Regenerate them from the checked-in script when a lossless source is required.

Durations and intended use:

- `podflow-intro.wav`: 5 seconds, before the spoken opening.
- `podflow-transition.wav`: 1 second, between the quick-news block and the deep dive.
- `podflow-outro.wav`: 4 seconds, after the spoken closing.

No music bed is intended beneath spoken news segments.
